# LeadaLine — 60s Demo Voiceover Script

**Voice:** UK female — confident, polished, warm, clear, modern UK SaaS advert.
**Delivery:** paced at ~1.22× to keep the full narrative under 60 seconds across 10 scenes (the raw read
came to ~69s, which the brief flagged as a permitted reason to adjust). Wording kept
faithful to the approved script; three of the longest lines were tightened slightly.
UK spelling throughout ("enquiries").

| # | Scene | Line |
|---|-------|------|
| 1 | Opening | Meet LeadaLine — your AI Office Team for busy service businesses. |
| 2 | AI Receptionist | When a new customer gets in touch, your AI Receptionist responds instantly and captures every detail. |
| 3 | Qualify | The lead is qualified, summarised, and sent straight to your team, |
| 4 | Owner Summary | so you know who they are, what they need, how urgent it is, and what to do next. |
| 5 | CRM Tracking | And it's all logged in one place, so nothing gets forgotten. |
| 6 | Booking | If the customer's ready, your AI Booking Assistant drops the next step into your diary. |
| 7 | Follow-Up | If they go quiet, your AI Follow-Up Assistant keeps things moving with friendly, on-brand reminders. |
| 8 | Review | Once the job's done, your AI Review Assistant asks for feedback at the right moment — earning more five-star reviews. |
| 9 | Reporting | Then your AI Reporting Assistant shows what came in, what was booked, and the time your team saved. |
| 10 | CTA | LeadaLine captures more enquiries, responds faster, and turns interest into booked work. Book your fifteen-minute demo call today. |

*Nothing implies cold calling — every line is about inbound enquiries, warm leads,
follow-up, reviews and reporting.*
