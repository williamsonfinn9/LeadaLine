What the audit reviews

This is not a website grader. It looks at the full journey a customer takes when they try to give you work — and where that journey can leak.

Enquiry capture

Can customers actually reach you? Phone visibility, enquiry forms, click-to-call, WhatsApp and clear calls to action.

Response availability

What happens when nobody answers — evenings, weekends, or while you're on the tools. Missed-call handling and out-of-hours routes.

Lead qualification

Whether enquiries arrive with the service, location and urgency attached — or need chasing before anyone can quote.

Follow-up process

How quotes and warm enquiries are chased. Most quoted work is won or lost purely on who follows up.

Visibility & organisation

Where leads live, whether statuses are tracked, and whether you can see what's working without digging through inboxes.

Questions

Who is this for?

UK contractors and owner-led service businesses: electricians, plumbers, roofers, builders, property maintenance, HVAC, waste removal, landscaping, facilities management, security and fire safety, cleaning and similar trades.

What do I actually receive?

A personalised web report: an overall score out of 100, category scores, what's working, where enquiries may be slipping through, an estimated commercial opportunity range, and the specific LeadaLine setup we'd recommend for your business.

How accurate is it?

Website findings are genuine observations of your public site. Everything about your internal process comes from your own answers. Where something can't be verified, the report says so — it never guesses.

Do you crawl my whole website?

No. We review a small number of public pages — typically your homepage, contact and services pages. We never access anything behind a login and we respect access restrictions.

Is this a sales pitch?

The report is useful on its own — you could act on every recommendation without us. If the findings resonate, you can book a demo where we show the recommended setup built around your business. That part is entirely up to you.

What happens to my data?

We process your details to produce your audit and store them securely so you can revisit your report. We only send follow-up marketing if you opt in, and every such email has an unsubscribe link. Contact hello@leadaline.com to have your data deleted at any time.
